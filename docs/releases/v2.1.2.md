# Dunzo Club v2.1.2

15 November 2026

The app requests fresh table state when you come back online or return to the app. Acknowledgments restore controls even if the table update arrived first. Definitively rejected bets are cleared, and rate-limited requests back off. A newer authoritative snapshot frees the controls once an old bet can no longer apply. Retries reuse the original command ID.

When it's your turn, your legal controls and community cards are available while the table animates.

Refresh the browser tab or close and reopen the installed app to load the update.
